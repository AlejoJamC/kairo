# kairo-logo

## Purpose

The logotype: the symbol next to the word "Kairo".

## Variants

- Horizontal: symbol on the left, word on the right. The word is 72% of the symbol height and the gap 21%.
- Stacked: symbol above, word below. The word is 60% of the symbol height and the gap 11.5%.
- On accent: the word in `text.onAccent` on `accent.default`; the symbol keeps its colors.
- The word is live text: "Kairo" with a capital K, Inter 600, letter-spacing −0.045em, line height 1, `text.primary`. It needs the product's font. The name is written "Kairo" everywhere, in the logotype too; it is never set in lowercase.
- The symbol follows kairo-mark: five layers from 32px tall, the cut below.
- Minimum size and clear space: Not defined in the approved design.

## States

- **default**: As described in Variants.
- **hover**: Not applicable.
- **active**: Not applicable.
- **disabled**: Not applicable.
- **loading**: Not applicable.
- **error**: Not applicable.

## Accessibility

- The word is the accessible name; the symbol is decorative. A link around the logotype takes its name from "Kairo".

## Domain value

Brand in the public site header, footer, auth and wizard headers.

## Platforms

- **web**: Public web.
- **desktop**: Not used: the app shows the symbol and the product name in the sidebar.
- **mobile**: Public web: designed, below 720px.
