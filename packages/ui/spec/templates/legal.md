# legal

## Purpose

Privacy policy and terms of service.

## Variants

- Privacy / Terms tabs, d2 title, last updated and version placeholders, table of contents and article; summary card first; pending legal text in bracketed placeholders.

## States

- **default**: As described in Variants.
- **hover**: Not applicable.
- **active**: Current section in the table of contents.
- **disabled**: Not applicable.
- **loading**: Not applicable.
- **error**: Not applicable.

## Accessibility

- Table of contents is a `<nav aria-label>`; current tab uses `aria-current`.

## Domain value

No invented legal text: everything not approved is a placeholder.

## Platforms

- **web**: Public web (no session).
- **desktop**: Not part of the desktop app design.
- **mobile**: Designed. Below 720px the page margin is 16px, grids collapse to one column and hide-sm elements are hidden.
