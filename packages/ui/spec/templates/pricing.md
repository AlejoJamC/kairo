# pricing

## Purpose

Pricing page.

## Variants

- Hero with billing period segmented (Annual / Monthly), four plan cards (Free, Pro recommended, Scale, Enterprise), currency note, comparison table, FAQ, footer.

## States

- **default**: Annual billing selected.
- **hover**: Not applicable.
- **active**: Monthly billing selected.
- **disabled**: Not applicable.
- **loading**: Not applicable.
- **error**: Not applicable.

## Accessibility

- Billing toggle uses `aria-pressed`.

## Domain value

Prices and currency are placeholders in the approved design.

## Platforms

- **web**: Public web (no session).
- **desktop**: Not part of the desktop app design.
- **mobile**: Designed. Below 720px the page margin is 16px, grids collapse to one column and hide-sm elements are hidden.
